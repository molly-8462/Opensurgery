About OpenSurgery

Research should not require piecing together a hundred disconnected posts.

OpenSurgery is a community-maintained surgeon directory and patient-experience archive designed around transgender patients’ research needs.

Mission

Our goal is to make factual surgeon information, procedure terminology, and first-person experiences easier to find without pretending that one person’s outcome predicts another’s.

How it works

Surgeon Info pages contain sourced, revision-tracked facts. Reviews contain structured firsthand accounts and optional photo timelines. Talk pages are for discussing improvements to factual articles. Keeping these systems separate helps readers understand what kind of evidence they are looking at.

Editorial model

Community members propose sourced changes. Published revisions preserve contributor attribution and can be restored without deleting later history. Reviews and uploaded images go through moderation focused on consent, privacy, spam, and policy compliance—not whether a person’s medical experience is flattering.

Independent project

OpenSurgery is an independent project. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Wikipedia, the Wikimedia Foundation, any surgeon, clinic, or medical association. Familiar reference-site conventions are used only to make research understandable.
