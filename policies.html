Public policy

How content is published and moderated

These prototype policies establish the standards the production service will need to make enforceable.

Editorial policy

Surgeon-page facts should use reliable public sources, neutral wording, and effective dates where information changes over time. Credentials, discipline, complications, and death require especially strong sourcing. Surgeons may report factual errors but cannot control patient reviews.

Review and photo policy

Reviews must describe the contributor’s own experience. Photos require publication rights and must not identify another person without documented consent. A “1-year update included” label means only that an approved image was identified by the reviewer as taken at least one year after surgery; it does not verify authenticity or outcome quality.

Written reviews never require photographs. Surgical imagery is hidden until the reader chooses to show it.

Talk page guidelines

Talk pages exist to improve factual Info content. Stay focused on wording, sources, and editorial decisions. Do not post medical advice, patient reviews, private information, threats, or attempts to contact a surgeon.

Revision policy

Published versions are immutable historical records. Undo and rollback create a new revision rather than erasing history. Private moderator notes and sensitive removed content are not exposed in public revision snapshots.

Advertising policy

Advertising is clearly labeled and separated from community content. Sensitive behavior—including transgender status, procedures researched, reviews, messages, photos, or health information—is not used for targeting. Payment cannot affect page content, review ordering, or moderation.
