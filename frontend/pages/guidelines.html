Community guidelines

Help people research without making the space unsafe.

Be respectful

No harassment, hate, threats, demeaning comments about bodies or outcomes, or attempts to organize brigading. Disagreement about sources is not permission to attack contributors.

Protect privacy

Do not post legal names, addresses, travel details, contact information, medical records, or images belonging to someone else without documented permission.

Describe your own experience honestly

Do not impersonate patients, fabricate reviews, coordinate promotional content, or present hearsay as firsthand experience. Clearly identify uncertainty and approximate dates.

Disclose conflicts of interest

Surgeons, staff, marketing agencies, and anyone receiving compensation must use the appropriate account type and disclose the relationship. They may not pose as patients.

Enforcement and appeals

Moderators may warn, limit, suspend, or remove access depending on severity and history. Content decisions and account sanctions should have a documented appeal path.
