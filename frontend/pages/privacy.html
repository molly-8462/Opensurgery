Privacy approach

Collect less. Explain clearly. Default to pseudonyms.

This prototype does not transmit or store form data. These are the privacy requirements for the eventual production service.

Principles

OpenSurgery treats procedure history, surgery dates, messages, contact information, and photographs as sensitive even where a law may classify them differently. The service should collect only what the feature needs and document retention periods.

Public content

Display names, public profiles, published reviews, Talk comments, and edit attribution are public. Users may choose a pseudonym and omit an approximate location or procedure history from their profile.

Private data

Email addresses, passwords, drafts, private messages, blocks, and private moderation notes are not public. Notification emails should link back to the site without including private message contents.

Photos

Uploads should have metadata removed, be decoded and safely re-encoded, and use generated storage identifiers. Users control whether an image is a draft, members-only, or public, subject to moderation and consent requirements.

Your control

Accounts should support data export, contact controls, deletion requests, blocking, and granular notification settings. Some security and abuse records may require a documented retention period.
