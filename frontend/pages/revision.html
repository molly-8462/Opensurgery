Mara Voss is a fictional American plastic and reconstructive surgeon based in Portland, Oregon. Her listed practice focuses on gender-affirming chest surgery and related revisions.[1]

Voss practices at the fictional Northbank Reconstructive Center, which reports accepting adult patients from within and outside Oregon.

Practice

Voss is listed as a surgeon at Northbank Reconstructive Center in Portland. Public information from the practice describes a focus on gender-affirming and reconstructive care. The Portland clinic reports a step-free main entrance.[1]

Procedures and techniques

Chest masculinization

Clinic materials list double-incision and periareolar approaches to chest masculinization. Technique selection depends on anatomy, health history, and the patient’s goals.

Chest revision

Patient-submitted sources describe scar and contour revisions for existing patients and some external referrals.
