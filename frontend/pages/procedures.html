Procedure guide

Explore procedures and techniques

A plain-language starting point for understanding terminology used across surgeon profiles and patient reviews.

This is not medical advice.Names and technique definitions vary between surgeons and countries. Eligibility requires an individual clinical assessment.
01

Chest procedures

Procedures involving chest shape, breast tissue, nipples, and related revisions.

Chest masculinization

May include removal or reshaping of breast tissue and repositioning or resizing the nipple-areola complex.

Find surgeonsCommon terms: double incision, periareolar, buttonhole

Breast augmentation

Uses implants, fat transfer, or both to increase breast volume and shape.

Find surgeonsCommon terms: implant, fat grafting
02

Genital procedures

Reconstructive procedures with varied goals, stages, and recovery paths.

Vaginoplasty

Creates vulvar structures and, depending on the approach, a vaginal canal.

Find surgeonsCommon terms: penile inversion, peritoneal, zero-depth

Phalloplasty and metoidioplasty

Different approaches to constructing or releasing a phallus, sometimes completed in stages.

Find surgeonsCommon terms vary by donor site and staging
03

Facial procedures

Individual or combined procedures intended to alter facial contours.

Facial feminization surgery

An umbrella term that may include forehead, jaw, chin, hairline, or nose procedures.

Find surgeonsPlans vary substantially by patient
04

Voice procedures

Surgical and non-surgical care intended to alter pitch or other voice characteristics.

Voice surgery

Several techniques may alter vocal-fold tension, length, or mass. Voice therapy may be recommended before or after surgery.
