Mara Voss is a fictional American plastic and reconstructive surgeon based in Portland, Oregon. Her listed practice focuses on gender-affirming chest surgery and related revisions.[1]

Voss practices at the fictional Northbank Reconstructive Center, which reports accepting adult patients from within and outside Oregon.[2] Availability, insurance participation, and consultation requirements may change and should be confirmed with the practice.

Practice edit

Voss is listed as a surgeon at Northbank Reconstructive Center in Portland. Public information from the practice describes a focus on gender-affirming and reconstructive care. The clinic reports offering virtual intake appointments before an in-person surgical consultation.[1]

The practice website lists English and Spanish among the languages available to patients and describes the main entrance as step-free.[2]

Procedures and techniques edit

The procedures below have appeared in current practice materials or community-submitted sources. Inclusion does not indicate that a technique is available to every patient.

Chest masculinization

Northbank materials list double-incision, periareolar, and buttonhole approaches to chest masculinization. The practice states that recommendations depend on anatomy, health history, and the patient’s goals.[1]

Chest revision

Patient-submitted sources describe scar, contour, and nipple-areola revisions for existing patients and some external referrals. This information has not yet been confirmed in current public practice materials.[3]
